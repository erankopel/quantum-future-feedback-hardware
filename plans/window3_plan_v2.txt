A4  COST MODEL CORRECTED. The registered model
    cost = N * (0.0121 + 2.577e-4 * shots) was said to be good to

    charges now recoverable from the two earlier accounts, it
    UNDER-predicts by up to 5.7 per cent. Replacement:
        cost = N * (0.005748 + 2.676436e-4 * shots) * 1.05
    max under-prediction 4.4 per cent before the factor, zero
    after it. Constants updated in rung11b, rung10 and rung8, and
    every budget guard now tests the safety-loaded figure. The
    registered ledger was optimistic by 5 to 9 per cent; the
    ledger below is priced on the corrected model.

A5  NEW ITEM W3-0b, COST PING, ~3.5 s. 12 circuits (the z+/z-
    sextet of the real tomography design) x 1024 shots, submitted

A6  THE V-FIT IS THE WRONG SHAPE. The correction is inserted at
    the same 15 (21,36) CZ sites that carry the error, so it
    propagates through the same interleaving and cancels at one
    step whatever the transfer factor. The response is therefore
        angle(x) = sqrt( p^2 + q^2 (x - x0)^2 )
    a hyperbola, a rounded V. The registered code fits a
    PARABOLA to all five arms. Monte Carlo at the measured noise
    floor, 3000 trials per point, true optimum -2.84 deg:
        5-arm parabola   -3.02 to -3.07 deg, sd 0.32 to 1.23
        hyperbola        -2.83 to -2.87 deg, sd 0.17 to 0.76
    The parabola is biased by +0.2 deg and is 1.5 to 2 times
    looser. Replacement, in w3_vfit.py: hyperbola PRIMARY,
    parabola on the three bracketing arms (k = 0,-1,-2)
    SECONDARY, 5-arm parabola retained as a reported diagnostic.

A7  GATE G-VZ HAD NO SIGMA. The gate says "3 sigma" and nothing
    computed one. Sigma is now a parametric bootstrap on the
    click probabilities, 400 draws, re-running the full explorer
    bundle. Measured floor at 1024 shots, from data in hand:
        sd(angle) 0.60 deg, sd(eta) 0.0032, sd(eta') 0.0033.

A8  NEW PRIMARY STATISTIC: THE SIGNED-ARM ASYMMETRY.
        ASYM1 = angle(+1) - angle(-1),  ASYM2 = angle(+2)-angle(-2)
    Under the null the +k and -k arms are exchangeable, so the
    expectation is exactly zero and drift enters both equally.
    Power at the measured floor:
        f_z    V2 as registered    ASYM1        ASYM2
        0.50         2%             72%         100%
        0.70        23%            100%         100%
        0.90        92%            100%         100%
    where f_z is the cosine between the round's rotation axis and
    Z, the only component a virtual Z can cancel.

A9  f_z IS ALREADY MEASURED, AND THE SCAN IS POWERED. From the
    rung2 baseline, which is the very circuit W3-1 runs:
        rung2 baseline  angle 6.67 deg   f_z 0.919 +/- 0.036
        rung6 plain     angle 28.47 deg  f_z 0.987 +/- 0.003
        rung6 echo      angle  9.27 deg  f_z 0.515 +/- 0.056
    At f_z = 0.92 the registered V2 has about 92 per cent power
    and ASYM has 100 per cent. W3-1 is powered as written at
    1024 shots; the shot count is NOT raised.
    NOTE FOR THE MANUSCRIPT, free finding: the echo arm's
    residual is only half Z-type. The echo removes the Z-type
    rotation and leaves a non-Z remainder, so W3-1 tests the
    PLAIN round's Z-type rotation and does not by itself address
    the echo-surviving residual. That distinction must be kept in
    the text.

A10 PREDICTION V2's SIGN CLAUSE IS WITHDRAWN AND RE-ISSUED.
    Registered V2 asserted the minimum is displaced to NEGATIVE
    k. The evidence already in hand points the other way:
      the round's measured rotation axis has n_z = -0.919, i.e.
        a Z rotation of -6.13 deg;
      register 14's per-gate CZ phase is H(ZI) = -2.84 deg, the
        SAME sign, so the transfer factor is positive and
        15 * kappa * 2.84 = 6.13 with kappa = 0.144;
      qiskit's rz(+t) = exp(-i t Z/2) ADDS +t to the Bloch
        rotation about +Z.
    Cancelling -6.13 deg therefore needs POSITIVE k. V2 is now
    sign-neutral (the minimum is displaced from k = 0, direction
    recorded as an output) and the sign is re-issued as the
    sharper, riskier side-prediction
      V2P the minimum lies at k = +1.
    Either outcome is a clean measurement: a minimum at k = -1
    measures a NEGATIVE transfer factor, that is, the interleaved
    single-qubit layer reversing the frame between insertions.
    ASYM is likewise tested two-sided.

A11 THETA HAND-OFF IS NOW GUARDED. w3_theta.json fed rung8's
    corrected arm with no check at all. It is now written only if
    the gate passed, the run is not a control, there is no drift
    artefact, AND |theta_opt| is inside the scanned half-span.
    Verified against a synthetic end-to-end self-test with four
    cases: true effect at +k, null, reversed transfer at -k, and
    a half-size phase whose optimum falls at the grid edge. All
    four returned the correct path and the correct write/refuse.

A12 NEW ITEM W3-1b, THE WRONG-LINK CONTROL, ~63 s, PATH A ONLY.
    Three arms (k = 0,-1,+1) x 72 settings, 1024 shots, the SAME
    insertion machinery pointed at the OTHER link (21,22), which
    carries 9 of the round's 24 two-qubit gates, using the
    measured theta_opt rather than the nominal 2.84 deg.
    Pre-registered prediction:
      C1 ASYM on the control link is consistent with zero at
         under 3 sigma while ASYM on (21,36) is above it.
    Justification: register 14 puts the gauge-invariant
    eigenphase at 4.15 +/- 1.54 deg on (21,36), 2.69 sigma, and

    it, the claim is localisation: the correction works on the
    identified link and not on the other one. That is the
    difference between a correlation and the paper's actual
    claim, and it costs 63 s. It runs AFTER the G-VZ verdict, in
    the same calibration day, so it can use the winning theta.
    It is implemented as options on the already gate-validated
    rung11b (--link, --ks, --tag), not as new code.

A14 ADVERSARIAL REVIEW OF THE AMENDED CODE, 25 Aug 2026, and
    the defects it found. Everything below was found BEFORE any
    submission and is fixed and unit-tested in the committed
    code. Recorded because a pre-registration that hides its own
    near-misses is worth less than one that lists them.

    MONEY. rung10's per-chunk budget guard had no running total.
    SamplerV2.run is fire-and-forget, so svc.usage() cannot have
    billed the chunks already in flight; every iteration compared
    the SAME stale consumed figure against ONE chunk and the
    guard never fired. Simulated: starting from 430 s consumed it
    submitted all 8 chunks and reached 636.6 s against a 600 s
    hard budget. Fixed by summing in-flight estimates; the same
    simulation now stops after 2 chunks at 487.0 s. An up-front
    whole-design check and a per-chunk record write were added,
    the latter so an interrupted run does not lose its job ids.

    MONEY. rung10's defaults were still the register-14 reduced
    design (L<=4, 512 shots). Run as W3-2 with --maxL 8 but the
    old shot default it would have cost 272 s, 65 s over the
    budgeted 207 s, and Path B would have totalled 551 s. The
    defaults are now the W3-2 values, L<=8 at 384 shots, so
    "--pair 21,36" alone is the pre-registered design.
    rung10's CAP_TOTAL stays at 500, deliberately below the
    G-STAB cap of 550, to reserve 50 s for stability epochs.

    MONEY. w3_0b_costping.py had no budget guard at all. Added.

    SCIENCE. GATE GVZ's insertion assertion was a tautology:
    n_ins counted MATCHING GATES, not inserted rz, so it compared
    a number with itself. It passed with zero rz inserted, and it
    passed for a link carrying no two-qubit gate at all, in which
    case all five arms are byte-identical and the 106 s scan
    measures nothing while appearing to pass. The gate now
    asserts the rz DELTA and refuses n_link = 0 or theta0 = 0.
    Verified: --link 21,20 now aborts.

    SCIENCE. w3_theta.json's write condition diverged from the
    path: a V2-only result printed PATH B and wrote the theta in
    the same breath. Writing is now gated on the path being A.

    SCIENCE. The span guard used "<=" against the OUTER grid
    point, so an optimum sitting on the grid edge was written
    about half the time, which is exactly risk R5. It now
    requires half a grid step of clearance AND that the winning
    arm is not the edge arm. A first correction over-tightened it
    to the second-outermost grid magnitude, which is the
    PREDICTED optimum itself and rejected good runs; the unit
    test over eight seeds caught that too.

    SCIENCE. V1 tested hyp["slope"] > 0, which is |q| and is
    positive for any successful fit; V1 passed on a perfectly
    flat response. It now requires a V of significant DEPTH and a
    displaced minimum. V3's threshold collapsed to zero when a
    bootstrap sd was missing ("or 0"), passing on a wobble one
    thirty-second of the noise floor; sds now fall back to the
    pooled floor and a missing floor fails. V4 passed when both
    indices were undetermined, since None == None; both must now
    be determined.

    SCIENCE. The drift detector was rebuilt twice. It originally
    looked only at k = +/-1, so the textbook signature in the
    OUTER pair scored as a strong effect, and it only caught both
    arms FALLING. Pairwise thresholding was then tried and
    abandoned: it missed the artefact about one seed in eight and
    raised false alarms on genuine signed effects where noise
    nudged the far arm the same way. The committed detector is a
    symmetry decomposition of the whole curve about k = 0,
      odd   a(+k) - a(-k)           a signed phase lives here
      even  a(+k) + a(-k) - 2 a(0)  a |k|-tracking artefact does
    and declares drift when the even component is significant at
    3 sigma and the odd one is not. No coherent phase can produce
    a response that depends on the magnitude of the insertion and
    not on its sign.

    PLUMBING. The control guard keyed on the filename tag, not
    the link, so one forgotten --tag would have overwritten the
    main W3-1 job record and then written w3_theta.json from the
    wrong-link control. It now keys on the link recorded in the
    job file, and a submitted job record is never overwritten.
    rung8's corrected-arm gate existed only in the dry branch,
    was advisory even there, and hardcoded 15; it is now enforced
    on the submit path with the count derived from the circuit.
    The on-disk predictions list still carried the withdrawn
    "negative k" clause; it now carries the A10 text.

    UNIT TESTS. w3_gate_unittest.py runs six synthetic cases,
    each over eight noise seeds, asserting BOTH the path and the
    write/refuse decision: true effect at +k, reversed transfer
    at -k, null, half-step optimum at the grid edge, and a
    |k|-tracking artefact in each direction. ALL PASS. The
    earlier single-seed self-test passed only by luck of its
    seed, which is why the seeded version replaces it.

EXECUTION RECORD, appended 25 Aug 2026 after the window closed.
Amendments made DURING the run, each labelled by when it was made
relative to seeing data. Results are in window3_results.txt.

A1a  POST W3-0b, PRE W3-1. The ping charged 5 s against 3.36 s
     predicted, an apparent 1.49x. Diagnosed as a per-JOB
     intercept, not a price rise: the ping is 12 circuits and the
     previous smallest job in the series was 157. Re-fit on all
     20 charges with c0 = 2.30 s per job gave max under-prediction
     +1.8 per cent against +32.8 per cent without it, and the
     safety factor fell from 1.05 to 1.02. Consequence that would
     have bitten later: the intercept is paid once per CHUNK, so
     W3-2's eight chunks carry 18.4 s the old model priced at
     zero. rung10 now counts chunks. Ledger survived intact.

A1b  POST W3-1, PRE W3-1c. The theta hand-off guard keyed on the
     filename tag as well as the link, which would have blocked
     the re-centred repeat (a tagged run on the carrier link that
     MUST be allowed to write theta). Now keys on the link alone.

A1c  POST W3-1c, POST-HOC AND LABELLED AS SUCH. W3-1c returned a
     curve that bottoms on a FLOOR rather than turning over, so
     the edge-arm veto built for risk R5 was refusing a theta that
     was in fact measured, just measured to lie anywhere on a
     plateau. Added a rule separating the two by asking whether
     the best arm's neighbour agrees inside the noise. Scope is
     narrow by construction: it changes only which theta reaches
     rung8's corrected arm and touches NO pre-registered
     prediction test. theta = +8.52 +/- 2.84 deg, plateau
     midpoint; the hyperbola independently gave +9.68.

A1d  POST W3-1b, AUTHOR-APPROVED. Cadence changed from the
     registered four epochs at ~3 h spacing to nine epochs
     chained back to back, because the plain-arm anchors showed
     the round moving on a ~15 min timescale. At the registered
     spacing the entire transient would have fallen between two
     samples. Deviation logged in w3_epoch_driver.py.

WHAT THE PRE-REGISTRATION GOT RIGHT, AND WRONG.
  RIGHT  the five-arm scan rather than a single-theta A/B. The
         registered V2 sign was wrong (A10 caught it before the
         run, and the data confirmed the correction is at
         POSITIVE k); a single-theta test at the register-14 sign
         would have measured the rotation getting worse and been
         written up as a refutation. The scan made the sign an
         output and survived being wrong about it.
  RIGHT  interleaving the arms per setting. A recalibration fell
         INSIDE W3-1's execution window and the differential
         statistics did not care.
  RIGHT  risk R5. It fired exactly as described, and the span
         guard held.
  WRONG  V3, which predicted eta falls at the minimum while eta'
         stays flat. In W3-1 the correction removed rotation and
         left eta alone; in the epochs it removed both. Recorded
         as a failed prediction, not rewritten.
  WRONG  the 3 h cadence, see A1d.
  WRONG  the cost model, see A4 and A1a.
